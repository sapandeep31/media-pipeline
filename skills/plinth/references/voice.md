# Voice: the words on slides

## Rules
1. Say the specific thing. "Settles in 400 ms instead of 3 days" beats "dramatically faster".
2. Name a person. "A warung owner in Makassar" beats "users".
3. Verbs over nouns. "We verify it" beats "verification is performed".
4. Every number carries a source line. Without one, write `[source needed]` and tell the user.
5. Say it once. The body does not repeat the headline.
6. Sound like the presenter. Read it aloud; if nobody would say it in a meeting, cut it.

## Words to cut (the lint script checks these)
revolutionize, revolutionary, game-changer, seamless, cutting-edge, state-of-the-art, next-generation, next-gen, unlock, unleash, empower, leverage, harness, elevate, delve, robust, holistic, synergy, paradigm, landscape used as a metaphor, ecosystem when it is not literal, innovative, world-class, best-in-class, transformative, supercharge, streamline, effortless, "in today's fast-paced world", "it's worth noting", furthermore, moreover, "in conclusion", "at the end of the day", "the future of X", "not just X but Y".

Indonesian words flagged too: merevolusi, revolusioner, mutakhir, memberdayakan, inovatif, solusi terdepan, "di era digital ini", "tidak hanya ... tetapi juga", sinergi, holistik.

Swapping a flagged word for its synonym does not fix it. Replace the claim with the fact behind it.

## Rhythm
Mix short and long sentences in the speaker notes. A headline can be a fragment if it still makes a claim ("Three days to settle. Every time.").
