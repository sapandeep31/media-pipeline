# Craft: layout and type

## Canvas and grid
1280x720, 12 columns, 64px outer margin, 24px gutters. The headline sits top-left on the same baseline on every slide. Keeping positions consistent is most of what "clean" means.

## Type
- Two families at most: one sans for everything (Inter, Geist, IBM Plex Sans), optionally one mono for numbers and code.
- Scale: eyebrow 14px caps with +0.08em tracking; headline 44 to 56px at weight 600 with -0.02em tracking; body 24px; source line 14px muted.
- Left-align. Center only the title slide and single-number slides.
- Body lines stay under 60 characters.

## Color
An off-white or off-black background, never pure. One accent color, used for the single thing each slide wants the eye to land on. Gray does the rest. Contrast 4.5:1 or better.

## Charts
- The chart title is the claim ("Fees doubled after 2024"), not the metric name.
- One series in the accent, everything else gray. Label lines directly and drop the legend.
- No 3D, no pie with more than 3 slices, no gridline heavier than 1px at 10% opacity.

## Images
Product screenshots go in a plain frame with a soft shadow, cropped to the part being discussed. Documentary photos of the real users or place run to the slide edge (`section.media`) with a small credit; no posed stock photos of people shaking hands or pointing at screens.

## Motion
One transition style for the whole deck (fade, or none). Use builds only when showing the answer early would spoil the setup.

## Tells that make a deck look machine-made
- Every slide is a title plus 3 to 5 bullets of equal length.
- An icon in a colored circle beside every bullet.
- Gradient headings, glowing borders, purple-to-blue everywhere.
- "The Problem", "Our Solution", "Why Us" used as headlines.
- Three symmetric feature cards on slide after slide.
- Round numbers with no source (10x, 100%, $1T).
